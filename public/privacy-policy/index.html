Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Songle is a daily music guessing game. This page explains exactly what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it. Songle has no advertising, no analytics trackers, and sells no data to anyone.

You can play without an account

Signing in is optional. Without an account, your game progress is kept only in your own browser's local storage and never reaches our servers. Clearing your browser data erases it.

What we store when you sign in

Accounts are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). You may sign in with Google, X, GitHub, an email address and password, or as an anonymous guest. We receive and store:

  • A user ID assigned by Firebase
  • Your display name
  • Your email address, where the provider supplies one
  • Your profile picture URL, or a generated avatar if you have none
  • Which sign-in provider you used
  • Game statistics: games played, wins, current and best streak, points, the distribution of how many guesses you needed, and the date you last played

Passwords are never seen or stored by Songle. Firebase handles them, and social sign-ins never expose a password to us at all.

What we store when you play

  • Your result for each daily song: score, number of attempts, whether you won, the guesses you made, and the song's title and artist
  • For challenge rooms: the room name, the songs the host chose, and each player's score in that room

What is publicly visible

Songle has leaderboards, so some of this is readable by other players by design. Your display name, profile picture, points, streak, and daily results are public. Your email address is not shown on any leaderboard and is not visible to other players. If you would rather not be identifiable, change your display name in Account Settings or play as a guest.

Local storage in your browser

Songle stores a small amount of data in your browser under keys beginning with songle_, so your progress survives a page refresh and the game still works offline. These are not advertising cookies and are not shared with anyone. Clearing site data removes them.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and database hosting
  • Netlify — website hosting, which keeps standard server access logs
  • Apple iTunes Search API — song metadata and audio previews. Your browser requests these directly from Apple when you play or search.
  • DiceBear — generates a placeholder avatar image when your account has no profile picture

Each of these has its own privacy policy governing data it receives.

How long we keep it

Your profile and statistics are kept as long as your account exists. Daily score records are kept so that leaderboards and historical results remain accurate.

Deleting your data

Open Account Settings in the app and choose to delete your account. This removes your profile document and your Firebase authentication record. If you want anything else removed, or you cannot access your account, contact us at the address below.

Children

Songle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing. Contact us to exercise any of these.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated. Continuing to use Songle after a change means you accept the revised policy.
